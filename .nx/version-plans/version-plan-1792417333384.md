On Android 8 and later, `screen.screenshot()` captures what a TextureView or a SurfaceView shows, such as a GPU-rendered canvas, a video or a camera preview, instead of transparent pixels. An on-screen capture now copies what the window shows, so effects only the GPU renderer draws, such as elevation shadows and `filter` blurs, appear in it, and an Android baseline recorded without them needs recording again. An element partly off the screen is still drawn as before.
